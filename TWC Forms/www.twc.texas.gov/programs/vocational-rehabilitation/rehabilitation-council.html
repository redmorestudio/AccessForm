Rehabilitation Council of Texas

The Rehabilitation Council of Texas (RCT) advises the Texas Workforce Commission on the policy, scope and effectiveness of the Vocational Rehabilitation program on behalf of all Vocational Rehabilitation stakeholders.

Please visit the RCT website at src-texas.org for more information and opportunities to reach out to the RCT.
